Questions

Good to know.

Who are the programmes for?

Learners moving from Year 6 to Year 8, learners switching from a national curriculum to Cambridge, and families who school at home.

Do I need special software?

No. Classes and consultations run in your web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Can parents follow progress?

Yes. Parents and guardians can link their account to their child's.

What can I get consultancy on?

Education (school set-up, curriculum, quality) and business (strategy, operations, leadership).

How do I book a consultation?

Send a request describing what you need. We'll confirm a time and invite you to your private session.
