Our identity

Every symbol stands for something.

Our crest tells the story of the academy: a place that lights the educational path, guides learners with purpose, and empowers them toward excellence.

Reading the crest

Eight symbols, one story.

1

The Shield

Protection, strength, integrity and excellence. Learners enter a structured and secure environment where their development is valued and protected.

2

The Crown

Excellence, leadership, achievement and high standards. We aim to develop learners who aspire to outstanding academic and personal achievement.

3

The Open Book

Knowledge, learning, discovery and continuous education, the foundation of everything the academy offers.

4

The Globe

International outlook, global citizenship and worldwide opportunity. Learners are prepared for an increasingly global world, not only local opportunities.

5

The Graduation Cap

Academic achievement, progression and the successful completion of milestones, from foundational learning toward higher goals.

6

The Torch

Guidance, enlightenment, inspiration and wisdom. It carries the idea in our name: a light along each learner's journey.

7

The Laurel Wreath

Victory, honour, accomplishment and excellence. Around the shield, it celebrates every learner's progress.

8

Navy & Gold

Navy for trust, professionalism, stability and academic seriousness. Gold for excellence, prestige, achievement and opportunity.
