Admissions

Begin your Light-Way journey.

Apply online in minutes, track your application with your own reference number, and start learning from home.

How it works

From application to your first class.

  1. Create your accountSign up as a student, or as a parent or guardian applying and following along on a child's behalf.
  2. Choose your programmeYear 6–Year 8 Bridging, National Curriculum to Cambridge Transition, or Home Schooling.
  3. Submit your applicationAdd a few details and upload at least one supporting document, such as an ID, birth certificate or previous school record (PDF, PNG or JPG).
  4. Get your reference numberYou receive a reference such as LW-{{ current_year }}-0001 and can follow each stage in your portal: received, under review, decision, placement.
  5. Be placed with a teacherOnce approved, each student is placed with a teacher and invited to live classes. Learners don't need to arrange this themselves.
  6. Learn, liveJoin classes from any device, see your timetable and read your teacher's progress reports.
