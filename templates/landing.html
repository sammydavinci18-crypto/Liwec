Who we are

Guiding learners toward excellence, wherever they are.

Our mission

To empower every learner with flexible, high-quality and internationally oriented education that builds knowledge, character, confidence and resilience, while providing personalised pathways that prepare them to thrive in a changing world.

Our vision

To be a leading virtual academy that lights the way to academic excellence, global opportunity and lifelong success for every learner, wherever they are.

Our programmes

Year 6–Year 8 Bridging

Builds the strong foundations learners need before moving on to the Cambridge pathway.

National Curriculum to Cambridge Transition

A guided move from a national curriculum into Cambridge, with support at each step.

Structured, teacher-led learning that families can follow from home.

How it works

Three steps. The administrator is in charge of each.

1

Ask or apply

Students and teachers submit an application. Anyone else sends a consultation or meeting request.

2

The administrator decides

The administrator reviews each one, approves it, sets the time, and builds the guest list. Students are placed with a teacher.

3

Meet and learn

Invited guests wait in the lobby until the administrator admits them. Notes and recordings stay with the session.
