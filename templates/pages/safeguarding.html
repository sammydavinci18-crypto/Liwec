Safeguarding & privacy

A secure place to learn.

Like the shield on our crest, protection is built into how Light-Way works, not added on afterwards.

How we protect learners

Built-in safeguards.

Invitation-only sessions

Every class and consultation is scheduled by the academy. Only invited people can enter a session, and nobody is on the call until the host lets them in from a waiting room.

Verified teachers

Teachers submit supporting documents that are reviewed before they can teach. Students are placed with teachers by the academy, never chosen by strangers.

Private student records

Applications, reports and documents are visible only to the student, their confirmed parents or guardians, their teachers and school staff.

Confirmed family links

A parent or guardian can only see a child's school record once the link has been confirmed by the student or by school staff.

Open recording

A session is recorded only when the host starts a recording, and everyone in the room can see that it is happening. Recordings can be watched back only by the people invited to that session.

Protected documents

Uploaded IDs and certificates are stored privately and are never served as public files.

Encrypted connections

The site is served over HTTPS, and live video uses the encryption built into the browser's WebRTC technology.

Account protection

Passwords are stored securely using one-way hashing, and the academy can suspend any account that breaks the rules.

Our expectations

Our values of integrity and respect apply online as they do in any classroom: be honest, be kind, and treat others as you would want to be treated. Concerns about anything that happens on Light-Way can be raised with the academy at any time.
